# Inventory Dragons 🐉

**Minecraft Fabric mod where dragons live right in your inventory!**

Created by the kind dragon with a saddle (and his rider).

## Features (working)

### Right-click hatching
Hold any Dragon Egg and right-click → hatches into a dragon!

### Feeding & Leveling
Hold Dragon Treat + dragon → right-click to feed (+25 XP).
Level & XP stored in NBT + shown in tooltip.

### Passive Buffs
Dragons in inventory give continuous effects:
- **Fire** → Fire Resistance
- **Ice** → Resistance
- **Nature** → Regeneration
- **Wind** → Speed + Slow Falling
- **Shadow** → Night Vision
- **Light** → Glowing

### Summon & Recall (Dragon Whistle)
- Right-click whistle to summon a dragon as an entity
- Right-click again near it to recall it back into item form

### Ride & Fly!
- Right-click a summoned dragon to mount it
- WASD + Jump/Sneak to fly
- The dragon follows you when not ridden

### Combat & Element Abilities
- Dragons attack nearby hostiles
- Each type has a unique breath attack:
  - **Fire** — sets on fire
  - **Ice** — slows
  - **Nature** — poisons
  - **Shadow** — blindness + weakness
  - **Light** — glowing + high damage
  - **Wind** — strong knockback
- While flying: left-click (attack) to perform an area breath attack

### Death Protection
Your Inventory Dragons survive player death and return to you!

### Dragon Types
Fire • Ice • Nature • Shadow • Light • **Wind** + Baby

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21+
- Fabric Loader + Fabric API
