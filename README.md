# Inventory Dragons 🐉

**Minecraft Fabric mod where dragons live right in your inventory!**

Created by the kind dragon with a saddle (and his rider).

## Idea
Small dragons that can sit in your inventory slots. They can:
- Protect items from despawn
- Give buffs while in inventory
- Be fed and leveled up
- Hatch from special eggs
- Be summoned to help in combat!

## Current Content

### Dragon Types
- Fire Inventory Dragon
- Ice Inventory Dragon
- Nature Inventory Dragon
- Shadow Inventory Dragon
- Light Inventory Dragon
- Baby Inventory Dragon

### Eggs
- Inventory Dragon Egg
- Fire / Ice / Nature Dragon Eggs

### Special Items
- Dragon Treat (for leveling)
- Dragon Whistle (summon/recall)

### Systems
- Leveling system (1–10 levels with titles)
- Entity placeholder ready for AI and models
- Full language support

## Planned Features
- Real hatching mechanics
- Passive & active abilities per type
- Inventory protection
- Custom models, animations and sounds
- Breeding system

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21+
- Fabric Loader
- Fabric API
